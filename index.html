    How treats work

    Treats shape what Biscuit wanders to next, and drawing sales fund his adventures. These are the confirmed rates:

    • 1 like on Instagram = 1 treat
    • 1 comment = 3 treats — only your first comment on a post counts. Extra comments on the same post do not earn more treats.
    • 1 follow = 5 treats
    • 1 share = 5 treats
    • $1 donation = 20 treats
    • Pat on this site: 1 treat — and only 1 pat per user per day. Once you have patted Biscuit today, the pat button is off until tomorrow, so treats cannot be farmed by tapping it again and again.
    • Feed on this site: 1 treat from your pouch → 1 meal, +1 treat into his fund — and only 1 feed per user per day. Once you have fed him today, the feed button is off until tomorrow.
    • Give a treat: 1 treat from your pouch → 1 treat into his wander fund, +energy. Your pouch is limited, so this cannot be farmed either. Bulk treating: give 5, 10 or 25 treats at once, or a custom amount — always capped by what is in your pouch (you cannot give more than you hold, and the amount must be at least 1).
    • Drawings: tap a drawing to get your unique purchase ID, donate on GoFundMe for Wildlife Victoria (100% of donations go to Wildlife Victoria) with that exact ID in your donation message, then message @biscuit.wanders on Instagram with that ID and your email to receive the printable drawing by email. No dollar price is set here — choose your donation amount on GoFundMe. Drawings are self-print printouts to print at home. Personal use only — © BiscuitWanders, no copying or resale.
    • Treat packs: tap to get your unique purchase ID, donate on GoFundMe for Wildlife Victoria with that exact ID in your donation message, then message @biscuit.wanders on Instagram with that ID. A treat pack puts 100 treats in your pouch — they are yours to use when you want (Give a treat / Feed), and are not added to Biscuit's total until you spend them. No dollar price is set here — choose your donation amount on GoFundMe.
    • Charity: 100% of donations on Biscuit's GoFundMe go to Wildlife Victoria. Shop purchases are donations made on GoFundMe for Wildlife Victoria — there is no GoFundMe checkout or API on this site, and this site cannot verify a GoFundMe donation. The charity fund meter on this page only counts earlier in-site treat purchases.
    • No trips: there is no way to send Biscuit anywhere. He chooses his own wanders.
